Refund & Cancellation Policy

This policy outlines the guidelines regarding order cancellations, fee deductions, and refund processing at MoveKart.

Overview

MoveKart supports fair cancellation and refund handling for customers, driver partners and system initiated exceptions. Refunds are assessed based on booking status, driver movement, payment status and service fulfillment.

1. Order Cancellations By The Customer

You may cancel your booking free of charge before a delivery partner has been assigned or within 5 minutes of partner assignment, provided the partner has not arrived at the pickup location.

If you cancel after a delivery partner has travelled toward or arrived at the pickup site, a cancellation or waiting charge may apply based on the order state and the applicable fare returned before confirmation.

2. Driver Partner / System Cancellations

If a driver partner cancels due to inaccurate pickup details, prohibited cargo, or customer unresponsiveness at the pickup site, applicable wait-time or cancellation charges may apply.

3. Refund Eligibility

  • Overcharging / Technical Error: Double payment or payment gateway glitches.
  • Non-Fulfillment: Online-paid order cancelled by MoveKart due to driver unavailability.
  • Damaged or Lost Shipment: Eligible claims under MoveKart Parcel Protection Cover if selected at booking.

4. Refund Processing Timeline

  • MoveKart Wallet: Approved refunds are credited according to the wallet ledger.
  • Original Payment Method: Refund timing depends on the configured payment gateway and the bank or payment network.

For refund queries, share your Order ID and payment receipt with support@movekart.in.
