Privacy Policy

At MoveKart, accessible from https://movekart.in, we prioritize the privacy of our users, driver partners, and business clients. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect and how we use, protect, and process it.

Overview

MoveKart processes information required to provide logistics, transport, live tracking, payment, support, safety and compliance services across its website, applications and operational platform.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Identification: Name, phone number, email address, and billing or pickup addresses.
  • Location Data: Precise real-time GPS location during active bookings for users and delivery partners.
  • Vehicle & Identity Verification: Driver license, vehicle registration, government ID proofs and background check records.
  • Payment & Transaction Details: Payment methods, wallet transactions, order history and invoice records processed through compliant gateways.
  • Device & Usage Info: IP address, device model, operating system and app usage logs for diagnostics.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Assign nearby delivery partners and calculate accurate trip routes and fares.
  • Provide live shipment tracking links to senders and receivers.
  • Process payments, issue refunds and generate legal tax invoices.
  • Verify user and partner identities to prevent platform fraud and safety breaches.
  • Send transactional updates through SMS, WhatsApp or push notifications for active orders.

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

MoveKart does not sell or rent your personal data to third parties. Limited data is shared only where operationally required.

  • Delivery Partners: Pickup/drop details and contact information needed to execute an order.
  • Third-Party Service Providers: Cloud hosting, SMS gateways and payment processors bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal Obligations: Information required by law enforcement or regulatory authorities pursuant to valid legal process.

4. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard encryption, including SSL/TLS for data in transit, and restricted access protocols for data at rest. Operational data is retained only as long as necessary for legal, tax, accounting and service requirements.

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You may access, modify, or request deletion of your personal account data. Account deactivation and data purging requests can be sent to the Data Protection Officer at privacy@movekart.in.
