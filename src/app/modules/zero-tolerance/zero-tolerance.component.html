Zero Tolerance Policy

At MoveKart, safety, integrity, and strict legal compliance are non-negotiable. We maintain a strict Zero Tolerance Policy across our entire network, including users, delivery partners, vendors, and staff. Any violation of applicable laws, safety standards, or contractual terms may result in immediate platform suspension, permanent termination, and referral to law enforcement authorities where applicable.

1. Prohibited & Illegal Goods

MoveKart strictly prohibits the transport, storage, or distribution of illegal, hazardous, or restricted goods across intra-city and inter-city routes.

  • Illegal Substances & Narcotics: Drugs, illegal chemicals, or unauthorized prescription medications.
  • Weapons & Explosives: Firearms, ammunition, fireworks, explosives, or hazardous tactical gear.
  • Hazardous & Toxic Materials: Flammable liquids, toxic chemicals, radioactive materials, or biohazards.
  • Contraband & Stolen Goods: Unlicensed commercial imports, stolen property, or counterfeit merchandise.
  • Live Animals & Biological Waste: Livestock, pets, or unregulated biological samples.

Attempting to transport prohibited goods may result in immediate order cancellation, permanent account ban, forfeiture of goods to law enforcement, and formal police reports.

2. Driver & Delivery Partner Safety Standards

  • Substance Abuse: Zero tolerance for operating any vehicle under the influence of alcohol, drugs, or impairing substances.
  • Unlicensed Driving & Identity Theft: Only background-verified, legally licensed partners matching the registered profile may conduct deliveries.
  • Traffic Violations & Reckless Driving: Overspeeding, reckless driving, or operating unroadworthy vehicles can lead to instant termination.

3. Fraud, Exploitation & Financial Integrity

  • Payment Fraud & Chargebacks: Deliberate chargeback fraud, fraudulent wallet top-ups, or fake payment receipts can lead to account lockouts and legal recovery.
  • Cargo Theft & Tampering: Theft, unauthorized opening, or swapping of parcels can lead to legal prosecution.
  • Fake Bookings & Platform Manipulation: Fake orders or promo manipulation for financial gain is prohibited.

4. Harassment, Violence & Discrimination

Physical violence, verbal abuse, intimidation, or property damage against customers, drivers, or support staff is prohibited.

Sexual harassment, unwanted advances, stalking-like behavior, or discrimination based on caste, religion, gender, race, disability, or sexual orientation can result in permanent removal and legal reporting.

5. Violation Reporting & Investigation

  • In-App Safety Button: Tap Emergency / Report directly on your active order page.
  • Compliance Email: Send evidence, order ID, and details to compliance@movekart.in.
  • Emergency Hotline: Call the 24/7 Priority Safety Helpline via the MoveKart app.

All reports are investigated confidentially and impartially within 24 hours.

6. Consequences of Violation

  • Immediate account suspension or deactivation.
  • Forfeiture of platform earnings or refunds associated with fraudulent or illegal activity.
  • Formal civil or criminal legal action under applicable local and national transport regulations.
  • Cooperation with law enforcement, including disclosure of GPS logs, identity documents and communication records where legally required.

MoveKart reserves the right to amend or update this policy at any time to align with statutory regulations. Continued use of the MoveKart platform constitutes acceptance of these terms.
