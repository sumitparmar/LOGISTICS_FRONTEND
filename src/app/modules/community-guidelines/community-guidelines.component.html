Community Guidelines

MoveKart is built on mutual trust, respect, and safety. These Community Guidelines apply to all platform users, including customers, business clients, and delivery partners.

Overview

Every MoveKart user and partner is expected to follow platform policies, applicable laws, safety rules, and respectful communication standards while using MoveKart services.

1. Zero Tolerance for Harassment & Discrimination

Verbal abuse, offensive language, threats, intimidation, or disrespect toward drivers or support personnel can result in an immediate platform ban.

Discrimination based on caste, religion, race, gender, disability, language, or any protected status is strictly prohibited.

2. Safety & Cargo Compliance

  • Accurate Goods Declaration: Customers must accurately describe cargo size, weight and type.
  • Prohibited Items: Illegal drugs, weapons, explosives, hazardous chemicals, stolen property and live animals are prohibited.
  • Vehicle & Driver Safety: Delivery partners must follow traffic laws, wear safety gear and never drive under the influence.

3. Fair Usage & Platform Integrity

Users and partners must complete orders and payments through the MoveKart app to preserve protection, invoicing and support coverage.

Fake accounts, unauthorized promo abuse, fraudulent chargebacks, or platform manipulation may result in permanent blacklisting and legal action.

4. Reporting Violations

Report safety or conduct concerns through the Emergency / Report option in the MoveKart app or email safety@movekart.in. Violations may lead to temporary suspension, permanent deletion, or civil/criminal police reporting.
